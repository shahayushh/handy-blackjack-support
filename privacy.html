Privacy Policy

handy-blackjack for Apple Watch — Last updated September 2026

Summary

handy-blackjack does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and does not connect to the internet or any server. Everything the app does happens entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, handy-blackjack does not:

  • Collect or transmit any personal information
  • Use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs
  • Access your location, contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Require or support account creation or sign-in
  • Store any data outside of your own device
  • Share data with any third party, because none is collected

Network access

The app makes no network requests of any kind. All gameplay — dealing, hitting, standing, splitting, and hand outcomes — is computed locally on your Apple Watch.

About the app

handy-blackjack is a card-counting and basic-strategy practice tool. It contains no betting of any kind — no chips, wagers, currency, odds, or payouts. It exists purely to practice counting cards and basic strategy, and is suitable for a general audience.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from any user, this policy applies equally regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future update adds functionality that does involve data collection — this page will be updated to reflect that before the change ships, and the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the support page.
